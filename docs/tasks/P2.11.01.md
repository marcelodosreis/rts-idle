## Contract

- `HEAL` targets the issuing player's own unit or another allied unit; buildings and enemy units are invalid targets.
- The caster must be a Monk. The target must have valid, non-full health.
- Healing restores 25 HP, capped at the target's max HP, with no Minerals or Energy cost.
- Healing range is 3 tiles using fixed-point distance. A Monk outside range moves to the target before casting.
- A successful cast starts a 160-tick cooldown (8 seconds at 20 simulation ticks per second).
- The Monk enters the healing order/animation while casting; the target receives a heal effect event and visible feedback.
- Invalid target, full health, unavailable Monk, out-of-range command state, and cooldown rejection are authoritative command errors.
- Heal state, cooldown, events, snapshots, restore, hashes, and replay remain deterministic.
- The player reaches the feature in the match HUD by selecting a Monk and issuing Heal on an allied damaged unit or the Monk itself.
- Browser E2E covers the real server flow in Chromium and Firefox.

## Design

- Keep the authoritative mutation in `packages/simulation`; shared and protocol packages contain typed command/event/snapshot contracts only.
- Keep ability rules in a typed game-data definition and use exhaustive dispatch for command and event variants.
- Keep renderer feedback event-driven: the Monk plays `heal` and the target receives `heal effect` without mutating simulation state.
