# P3.10.01 - Projectiles

## Task

- ID: `P3.10.01`
- Objective: Replace applicable instant ranged damage with authoritative, observable projectiles.
- Why: Ranged combat needs readable travel, impact, dead-target, and expiry behavior.
- Scope: `packages/game-data`, `packages/shared`, `packages/protocol`, `packages/simulation`, `apps/server`, `packages/renderer`, `apps/web`, and E2E tests.
- Non-goals: Area damage, new faction systems, terrain interception, and AI.

## Contract

- Projectile creation, movement, impact, dead-target handling, and TTL are deterministic and snapshot-safe.
- Homing and hidden trajectory data remain server-private; the client receives only the observable segment.
- Terrain does not intercept projectiles in the MVP, as defined by the master plan.
- Natural interaction: select a ranged unit and issue an attack against a visible target.
- Success feedback: the player sees projectile launch, travel, impact, and resulting damage.
- Blocked/error feedback: out-of-range, hidden, dead, and expired targets produce readable non-damage feedback.
- Browser target: `tests/e2e/match/projectiles-playable.spec.ts` through the real server.

## Design

Keep projectile state in simulation and project only observable data. Preserve the frozen system order, canonical snapshots, typed projectile tags, and renderer ownership of animation.

## Tests and validation

- Unit and simulation tests for creation, movement, impact-once, dead targets, TTL, and serialization.
- Determinism and replay tests for projectile sequences.
- Browser E2E in Chromium and Firefox covering launch, travel, impact, and blocked shots.
- `pnpm run verify`

## Player-facing completion

- [ ] The player can reach ranged combat through the match screen.
- [ ] The player can issue a natural ranged attack.
- [ ] Launch, travel, impact, damage, and blocked states are visible.
- [ ] The real server path is covered by browser E2E.

## Acceptance and stop conditions

- [ ] Every impact applies at most once.
- [ ] Dead targets and TTL cleanup are safe and deterministic.
- [ ] The player-facing flow and E2E pass in both required browsers.
