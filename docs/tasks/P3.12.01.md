# P3.12.01 - Combined Army and Chokepoint Stress

## Task

- ID: `P3.12.01`
- Objective: Deliver a repeatable playable army scenario combining navigation, collision, visibility, projectiles, and area damage.
- Why: The phase needs evidence that the integrated game remains playable under sustained pressure.
- Scope: `packages/simulation`, `apps/server`, `packages/renderer`, `apps/web`, scenario fixtures, benchmarks, and browser tests.
- Non-goals: Multiplayer rooms, AI strategy, new pathfinding algorithms, and unrelated content expansion.

## Contract

- The scenario uses a fixed seed and authored commands through the real server.
- Units navigate a chokepoint without overlap, deadlock, hidden-state leakage, or nondeterministic combat outcomes.
- The match screen shows progress, blocked/recovery states, vision transitions, projectile impacts, and area damage.
- Natural interaction: the player selects groups, issues movement and combat commands, and observes the scenario resolve.
- Success feedback: the army advances through the chokepoint and the final result is visible.
- Blocked/error feedback: persistent blockage is reported and the scenario does not silently stall.
- Browser target: `tests/e2e/regression/regression-army-chokepoint-playable.spec.ts` through the real server.

## Design

Reuse existing authoritative systems and the established regression scenario. Keep the benchmark fixture deterministic and separate from renderer-only timing.

## Tests and validation

- Simulation tests for progress, no overlap, no deadlock, deterministic replay, and fixed-seed outcome.
- Benchmark evidence for the agreed performance thresholds.
- Browser E2E in Chromium and Firefox covering command input, progress, combat feedback, and final result.
- `pnpm run verify`

## Player-facing completion

- [ ] The player can reach the stress scenario through the match screen.
- [ ] The player can issue the required movement and combat commands.
- [ ] Progress, recovery, combat, blockage, and final result are visible.
- [ ] The real server path is covered by browser E2E.

## Acceptance and stop conditions

- [ ] The scenario makes progress without overlap or deadlock.
- [ ] Same seed and commands produce the same result and hash.
- [ ] Performance thresholds and the complete player-facing browser flow pass.

## Current evidence

- The deterministic simulation stress fixture, no-overlap/water/footprint and
  deadlock checks, tick-by-tick replay comparison, and snapshot/restore
  continuation are implemented and pass (`tests/simulation/army-chokepoint.test.ts`).
- The stress fixture exposed and fixed an avoidance bug that allowed units to
  enter static obstacles; see
  `docs/postmortems/2026-11-01-avoidance-static-obstacle-bypass.md`.

  Measured on the local Ryzen 5 2600 reference run: median p95 116.17 ms for
  500 entities, which does not meet the 10 ms gate. This packet remains open.
- The browser stress flow (`tests/e2e/regression/regression-army-chokepoint-playable.spec.ts`)
