## Contract

- The only resource is Minerals; Pawns gather and deposit at owned completed Castles.
- `BASE` becomes canonical `CASTLE`; `SUPPLY_DEPOT` becomes canonical `HOUSE`.
- A Castle has an individual Tier. Castle I upgrades to Castle II using the current Castle cost and construction duration, occupying its Pawn production queue while upgrading.
- Castle II content is globally available after at least one owned Castle completes Tier II. Losing every Castle II blocks new Tier II construction but does not disable existing Tier II buildings, unlocked Lancer/Monk production, active research, or completed research.
- A completed owned Monastery uses one shared FIFO queue for Monk training and Research, with at most five items; only the first item is active and the next item cannot advance until it is removed.
- Researches are single-level and globally unique per player. The same research cannot be active or queued in two Monasteries.
- `RESEARCH` atomically validates ownership, completed Monastery, Tier II access, queue capacity, uniqueness, and Minerals before reserving the cost.
- `CANCEL_RESEARCH` removes a queue item by canonical index and refunds using the existing production refund rule.
- Monk and Research queue entries share the same snapshot order and HUD queue. `CANCEL_PRODUCTION` only removes queued Monk entries; `CANCEL_RESEARCH` may remove active or queued Research entries by that shared index.
- Attack costs 150 Minerals for 30 seconds and adds 2 effective damage to units with `military_attack_upgrade`.
- Defense costs 150 Minerals for 30 seconds and adds 1 armor to units with `military_defense_upgrade`.
- Economy costs 175 Minerals for 35 seconds and changes Pawn cargo capacity from 10 to 12 without changing gather cadence.
- Movement costs 175 Minerals for 35 seconds and adds 10% speed to Pawn, Warrior, Archer, Lancer, and Monk.
- Defense resolves damage as `max(1, incomingDamage - armor)`. Armor is projected in snapshots and the HUD.
- Warrior, Archer, and Lancer receive Attack. Warrior, Archer, Lancer, and Monk receive Defense. Pawn receives Economy and Movement. All listed units receive Movement as specified.
- Research state, queue progress, completed technologies, modifiers, and all new unit/building data participate in canonical snapshot/restore/hash and deterministic replay.
- The player-facing flow is `Gather → Deposit → Castle II → Monastery → Research → Produce/Use Units → Verify Modifier`.

## Design

- Closed domain values use shared `as const` registries and exhaustive dispatch.
- Research definitions and unit capabilities live in `game-data`; simulation derives effects without mutating static definitions.
- The simulation remains the only writer. Protocol and server expose independent projections only.
- The research system runs before combat so a completed research affects the documented tick deterministically.
- Movement modifiers use integer fixed-point arithmetic; no floating-point gameplay state is introduced.
- Canonical tag additions are append-only within the new schema, with a simulation-version bump and regenerated golden evidence.
