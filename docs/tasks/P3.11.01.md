# P3.11.01 - Area Damage

## Task

- ID: `P3.11.01`
- Objective: Add deterministic area damage with readable impact feedback and explicit target filtering.
- Why: The player needs a concrete, testable area-combat interaction rather than an isolated damage formula.
- Scope: `packages/game-data`, `packages/shared`, `packages/protocol`, `packages/simulation`, `apps/server`, `packages/renderer`, `apps/web`, and E2E tests.
- Non-goals: Generic faction ability frameworks, friendly fire, terrain interception, and AI.

## Contract

- The concrete producer is one selected Archer using the unit-specific
  `EXPLOSIVE_SHOT` command. No generic ability framework is introduced.
- The command targets one currently visible enemy unit or completed enemy
  building. It costs 25 Gold, has a 160-tick cooldown, and has a three-tile
  cast range. Admission is atomic and rejected commands change no state.
- An accepted cast launches an authoritative area projectile at 10 tiles/s with
  a 20-tick TTL. The source may die without cancelling it. If the target dies,
  it detonates once at the target's last known position; TTL expiry does not
  detonate and does not refund the cost.
- Damage is the Archer's effective attack damage captured at launch. Armor is
  applied independently to each target at impact after falloff, with a minimum
  of one damage for an affected target.
- Impact uses a center point, 100% damage within 0.5 tile, and 50% damage in the remaining radius.
- The total impact radius is 1.5 tiles. Both radius boundaries are inclusive;
  half damage rounds down before armor.
- Enemies only are valid targets and each entity receives one impact per explosion.
- Enemy units and completed enemy buildings are affected. Friendly entities are
  never damaged.
- Spatial queries are followed by exact-distance checks; ordering and hashes remain deterministic.
- Projectile IDs and affected entity IDs are processed in ascending order.
- Casting preserves the Archer's current order and never initiates pursuit.
- Natural interaction: select one Archer, activate `Explosive Shot` in the
  command bar, then right-click a visible enemy target. Escape cancels targeting.
- Success feedback: impact center, radius, affected targets, damage, and cooldown/resource result are visible.
- Blocked/error feedback: invalid range, cooldown, cost, hidden target, and friendly target are visibly rejected.
- Browser target: `tests/e2e/match/area-damage-playable.spec.ts` through the real server.

## Design

Register one Archer-specific definition in game-data and protocol. Reuse the
existing ability-cooldown, projectile, pending-damage, spatial-index, command
bar, and effect boundaries. Public projectile observations never expose target
IDs, captured damage, or hidden affected entities. A visible impact event may
contain its center and radii, but never affected entity IDs.

The authored `area-damage` setup contains two player Archers, exactly 25 Gold,
inner- and outer-radius enemies, one just-outside enemy, one friendly unit in
the radius, an out-of-range enemy, and a revealable enemy building. This makes
success, cooldown, insufficient-cost, range, friendly, and hidden-target states
reachable through normal match interaction.

Canonical projectile and cooldown state changes advance simulation version
`0.26.0` to `0.27.0`. Command, event, and snapshot wire changes advance
protocol version `0.10.0` to `0.11.0`.

## Tests and validation

- Unit and simulation tests for inner radius, outer radius, boundary distance, one-hit behavior, dead targets, and enemy-only filtering.
- Simulation tests cover cost, cooldown, range, visibility, atomic rejection,
  armor after falloff, source/target death, TTL, buildings, and stable ordering.
- Determinism, snapshot/restore continuation, and replay tests.
- Protocol tests for command input, cooldown projection, impact events, and malformed data.
- Renderer and web tests cover radius feedback, targeting mode, cost/cooldown
  controls, cancellation, and authoritative rejection presentation.
- Browser E2E in Chromium and Firefox covering activation, impact, cooldown/cost, and rejection.
- `pnpm run verify`

## Implementation boundaries

- Do not add a generic `CAST_ABILITY` command or generic effect schema.
- Do not add Siege, Factory, Energy, faction, terrain-interception, or AI content.
- Do not let the browser calculate targeting validity, damage, falloff, cooldown,
  cost, projectile homing, or affected entities.
- Do not change the frozen simulation pipeline order.
