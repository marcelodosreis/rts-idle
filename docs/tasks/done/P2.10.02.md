# P2.10.02 — Building Health and Shared Damage

## Objective

Give completed buildings authoritative health and route building damage through
the existing shared `Health`, pending-damage, and death systems.

## Contract

- Building max HP is defined centrally: Base 500, Barracks 400, Supply Depot 250.
- Foundations have no health and cannot be damaged until completion.
- Completed buildings receive full HP at the completion tick.
- Existing attack commands may target enemy completed buildings.
- Building destruction removes the entity and clears workers' stale BUILD orders.
- Building snapshots expose `hp` and `maxHp` when health exists.
- Buildings render the same health bar treatment as other world objects.

## Non-goals

- Repair commands and repair economics; those remain P2.10.
- Building armor, resistances, or construction damage.
- New building destruction event types.

## Acceptance

- Building definitions expose the authored max HP values.
- Completed constructions receive and serialize Health.
- Damage reduces and can destroy a building through the shared death system.
- Destroyed buildings do not leave BUILD orders or production reservations behind.
- Protocol, projection, renderer, simulation, and invariant tests pass.
