# P2.10.01 — Persistent Unit Health Bars

## Objective

Keep the authoritative unit health bar visible whenever a unit exposes valid
health, including at full health.

## Scope

- Preserve the existing HP ratio, colors, dimensions, and overlay position.
- Hide the bar only when HP data is unavailable or invalid.
- Cover full-health and missing-health renderer behavior.

## Non-goals

- Building health or repair behavior.
- New HUD controls or health balancing.

## Acceptance

- Full-health troops visibly render a complete green HP bar.
- Damaged troops retain the existing proportional/color behavior.
- Units without HP data do not render a health bar.
- Renderer unit tests pass.
